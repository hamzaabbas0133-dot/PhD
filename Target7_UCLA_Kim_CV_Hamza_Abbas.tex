\section{Profile}

Mechanical engineer and lecturer with hands-on expertise in
\textbf{digital microfluidics (DMF/EWOD), droplet actuation, and microfluidic
device fabrication}. As a Graduate Research Assistant at GIK Institute's Biofuels
\& Microfluidics Lab, I fabricated and characterised EWOD-based digital microfluidic
devices, investigating droplet actuation as a function of voltage and hydrophobic
coating selection. I also derived a modified Lucas--Washburn equation for capillary
wicking in heterogeneous porous substrates, validated to below 8\% error. I use
COMSOL Multiphysics for electric-field and flow simulation, and MATLAB/Python for
data analysis. Two journal manuscripts are under review at the \textit{Journal of
Fluid Mechanics} and \textit{Langmuir} (ACS), and I hold a co-authored patent
application for a capillary-driven flexible time-temperature indicator. I am seeking
a PhD position in the Micro \& Nano Manufacturing Lab, where my DMF fabrication and
EWOD characterisation experience directly aligns with Prof.\ Kim's research programme.

\section{Research Experience — Digital Microfluidics \& EWOD}

\textbf{Graduate Research Assistant} -- Biofuels \& Microfluidics Lab, GIK
Institute \hfill \textit{Feb 2022 -- May 2024}\\
\textit{Topi, Pakistan}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{EWOD device fabrication \& characterisation:} Fabricated digital
        microfluidic (DMF/EWOD) devices using photolithographic electrode
        patterning on PET substrates and thermal lamination; tested droplet
        actuation, splitting, and merging operations.
  \item \textbf{Droplet dynamics:} Investigated EWOD droplet actuation as a
        function of applied voltage and hydrophobic coating selection; characterised
        contact-angle response and droplet mobility (published,
        \textit{MATEC Web of Conferences}, 2024).
  \item \textbf{Dynamic contact-angle measurement:} Studied contact-angle variation
        with applied voltage and droplet volume in DMF devices (published,
        \textit{Engineering Proceedings}, MDPI, 2023).
  \item \textbf{DMF Lab-on-a-Chip:} Co-designed and fabricated a DMF device for
        lab-on-a-chip applications (published, \textit{Engineering Proceedings},
        MDPI, 2023).
  \item \textbf{Paper-based microfluidics:} Fabricated paper-based lateral-flow
        devices using CO$_2$ laser micromachining; derived and validated a modified
        Lucas--Washburn equation for heterogeneous porous substrates to below 8\%
        error.
  \item \textbf{Numerical modelling:} Used COMSOL Multiphysics to simulate
        electric-field distributions, porous-media flow, and heat transfer in
        microfluidic structures.
  \item \textbf{Imaging \& analysis:} Tracked droplet and wetting-front dynamics
        using ImageJ; performed model fitting using OriginPro and MATLAB.
\end{itemize}
