%--- PROFILE ---
\section{Profile}

Mechanical engineer and lecturer with a strong background in
\textbf{microfluidic device fabrication, complex fluid transport, and numerical
flow modelling}. My MS thesis derived and experimentally validated a modified
Lucas--Washburn equation for wetting-front propagation in stratified porous
assemblies with dissimilar permeabilities, achieving below 8\% prediction error.
I fabricate paper-based and digital microfluidic devices (CO$_2$ laser
micromachining, photolithography, EWOD, thermal lamination) and use COMSOL
Multiphysics for flow, electric-field, and heat-transfer simulations. Two
journal manuscripts arising from this work are currently under review at the
\textit{Journal of Fluid Mechanics} and \textit{Langmuir}. I am seeking a
PhD position in the James Weir Fluids Laboratory where my experimental
microfluidics and numerical modelling skills can support research into
complex-fluid flows and microfluidic transport phenomena.

\section{Research Experience — Microfluidics \& Flow Modelling}

\textbf{Graduate Research Assistant} -- Biofuels \& Microfluidics Lab, GIK
Institute \hfill \textit{Feb 2022 -- May 2024}\\
\textit{Topi, Pakistan}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Microfluidic device fabrication:} Fabricated paper-based lateral
        flow devices and digital microfluidic (DMF) chips using CO$_2$ laser
        micromachining, photolithographic electrode patterning on PET film, and
        thermal lamination; characterised devices using contact-angle measurements
        and fluorescence imaging.
  \item \textbf{EWOD actuation:} Implemented and tested electrowetting-on-dielectric
        (EWOD) droplet actuation; investigated the effect of voltage control and
        hydrophobic coating selection on droplet mobility (published,
        \textit{MATEC Web of Conferences}, 2024).
  \item \textbf{Capillary flow modelling:} Derived a modified Lucas--Washburn
        equation for fluid transport in heterogeneous porous assemblies; validated
        against experiments with below 8\% error across multiple substrate
        geometries, thickness levels, and fluid volumes.
  \item \textbf{Numerical simulation:} Used COMSOL Multiphysics to model porous-media
        flow, electric-field distributions, and heat transfer in microfluidic
        structures; compared simulation outputs against experimental wetting-front
        data (ImageJ tracking).
  \item \textbf{Complex-geometry transport:} Investigated flow in a leaf-inspired
        hierarchical channel architecture to characterise resistive flow mechanics
        (manuscript under review, \textit{Journal of Fluid Mechanics}).
\end{itemize}
