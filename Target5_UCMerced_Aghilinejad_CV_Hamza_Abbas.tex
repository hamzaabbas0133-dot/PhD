\section{Profile}

Mechanical engineer and lecturer with research expertise in
\textbf{experimental biofluid mechanics, microfluidic device fabrication, and
capillary flow modelling}. As a Graduate Research Assistant in GIK Institute's
Biofuels \& Microfluidics Lab, I fabricated and characterised paper-based and
digital microfluidic (DMF/EWOD) devices for controlled fluid transport studies,
and derived a modified Lucas--Washburn equation predicting capillary wicking in
heterogeneous porous substrates to below 8\% error. I use COMSOL Multiphysics for
flow and heat-transfer simulation, and MATLAB/Python for data analysis and model
fitting. Two journal manuscripts from this work are under review at the
\textit{Journal of Fluid Mechanics} and \textit{Langmuir} (ACS). I am seeking a
PhD position in the Aghilinejad Group, where my experimental microfluidics and
fluid-mechanics modelling background directly supports research in biofluid
mechanics and microfluidic particle separation.

%--- RESEARCH EXPERIENCE (LEAD) ---
\section{Research Experience — Biofluid Mechanics \& Microfluidics}

\textbf{Graduate Research Assistant} -- Biofuels \& Microfluidics Lab, GIK
Institute \hfill \textit{Feb 2022 -- May 2024}\\
\textit{Topi, Pakistan}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Capillary flow in confined geometries:} Derived a modified
        Lucas--Washburn equation for wetting-front propagation in stratified
        porous assemblies with dissimilar permeabilities; validated to below 8\%
        error across multiple substrate configurations and fluid viscosities.
  \item \textbf{Microfluidic device fabrication:} Fabricated paper-based lateral-
        flow and digital microfluidic (DMF/EWOD) devices using CO$_2$ laser
        micromachining, photolithographic electrode patterning on PET, and thermal
        lamination; tested devices for controlled fluid actuation and transport.
  \item \textbf{Particle and droplet dynamics:} Investigated EWOD droplet
        actuation as a function of voltage and hydrophobic coating; characterised
        droplet contact-angle response and mobility for diagnostic applications
        (published, \textit{MATEC Web of Conferences}, 2024).
  \item \textbf{Flow characterisation in complex geometries:} Studied resistive
        fluid transport in a hierarchical leaf-inspired channel architecture,
        linking structural geometry to flow resistance (under review,
        \textit{Journal of Fluid Mechanics}, 2025).
  \item \textbf{Numerical modelling:} Used COMSOL Multiphysics to simulate
        porous-media flow, electric-field distributions, and heat transfer in
        microfluidic structures; validated against experimental data.
  \item \textbf{Imaging \& analysis:} Tracked wetting fronts using ImageJ;
        performed statistical analysis and model fitting using OriginPro and
        MATLAB.
\end{itemize}
