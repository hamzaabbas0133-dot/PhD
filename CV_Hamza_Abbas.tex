interconnected areas: (1)~\textbf{industrial process simulation} of waste-heat recovery systems,

%--- RESEARCH EXPERIENCE ---
\section{Research Experience}

\noindent\textbf{Pillar 1 — Industrial Process Simulation: Waste-Heat Recovery Systems}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em, itemsep=1pt, topsep=3pt]
  \item Modelled an integrated ORC + VARS (Organic Rankine Cycle + Vapour Absorption
        Refrigeration System) for waste-heat recovery in a cement plant using Aspen Plus
  \item Achieved a combined system efficiency of \textbf{17.9\%}, demonstrating significant
        industrial energy recovery potential
  \item Optimised working fluid selection, heat exchanger sizing, and operating conditions
        through parametric simulation studies
\end{itemize}

\vspace{6pt}

\noindent\textbf{Pillar 2 — Porous Media \& Capillary Flow Modelling}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em, itemsep=1pt, topsep=3pt]
  \item Developed a modified Lucas-Washburn equation incorporating dynamic contact angle
        correction and inertial resistance to predict spontaneous wicking in engineered
        porous substrates
  \item Validated the model experimentally across a range of pore geometries and fluid
        viscosities, achieving $<$8\% prediction error
  \item Manuscripts currently under review: \textit{Langmuir} (ACS) and
        \textit{Journal of Fluid Mechanics} (Cambridge University Press)
\end{itemize}

\vspace{6pt}

\noindent\textbf{Pillar 3 — Microfluidics Fabrication \& Diagnostics}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em, itemsep=1pt, topsep=3pt]
  \item Hands-on experience with \textbf{CO$_2$ laser micromachining},
        \textbf{photolithography}, and \textbf{EWOD} (electrowetting-on-dielectric)
        platform fabrication
  \item Developed paper-based microfluidic diagnostic devices leveraging capillary-driven flow
  \item Designed and characterised microchannels for controlled flow experiments in support
        of capillary flow model validation
\end{itemize}
