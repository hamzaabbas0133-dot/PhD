%--- PROFILE ---
\section{Profile}

Mechanical engineer and lecturer specialising in \textbf{porous-media transport,
capillary flow modelling, and microfluidic device fabrication}. My MS thesis
derived and experimentally validated a modified Lucas--Washburn equation for
stratified porous assemblies with dissimilar permeabilities, achieving below 8\%
prediction error across a range of substrate geometries and fluid conditions. I
fabricate paper-based and digital microfluidic devices (CO$_2$ laser, photolithography,
EWOD) and model flow and heat transfer using COMSOL Multiphysics. I am applying for
the QUT PhD scholarship in Structural Capillarity and Engineered Porous Media, where
my capillary-flow modelling and experimental microfluidics skills directly support the
project's goals.

\section{Research Experience — Porous Media \& Microfluidics}

\textbf{Graduate Research Assistant} -- Biofuels \& Microfluidics Lab, GIK Institute
\hfill \textit{Feb 2022 -- May 2024}\\
\textit{Topi, Pakistan}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Capillary flow modelling:} Derived a modified Lucas--Washburn
        equation for wetting-front propagation in heterogeneous porous assemblies
        (stratified layers with dissimilar permeabilities) and validated it
        experimentally to below 8\% error across multiple substrate configurations.
  \item \textbf{Porous-media simulation:} Used COMSOL Multiphysics to model
        porous-media transport, electric fields, flow behaviour, and heat transfer
        in microfluidic and porous structures.
  \item \textbf{Capillary dynamics:} Investigated capillary flow in paper-based
        multilayer systems as a function of substrate thickness, contact area, and
        fluid volume; results form the basis of the \textit{Langmuir} manuscript
        under review.
  \item \textbf{Device fabrication:} Fabricated paper-based and digital
        microfluidic devices using CO$_2$ laser micromachining, photolithographic
        electrode patterning on PET, and thermal lamination.
  \item \textbf{Data analysis:} ImageJ for experimental wetting-front tracking;
        OriginPro for curve fitting and model validation.
\end{itemize}
